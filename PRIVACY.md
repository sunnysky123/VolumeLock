## 中文

### 1. 我们收集的信息

VolumeLock 不收集、不存储、不传输、不出售，也不共享任何个人信息。

具体而言，本应用：

- 不要求注册账号或登录；
- 不收集姓名、邮箱、电话号码、地址或其他个人身份信息；
- 不包含广告、分析、埋点、遥测或用户行为追踪；
- 不收集崩溃日志或诊断数据；
- 不读取、录制、保存或上传任何音频内容；
- 不向任何服务器发送数据。

### 2. 音频设备访问

为了实现扬声器和麦克风音量设置及锁定功能，VolumeLock 会在本地枚举 Windows 音频终结点，并读取、设置所选设备的系统音量。

应用可能声明麦克风设备能力，以便识别和调整麦克风端点音量；但本应用不会捕获、录制、保存或传输麦克风采集到的声音内容。

### 3. 本地处理

本应用的所有功能均在用户设备本地完成。音量设置、锁定状态和托盘交互不会离开设备，也不会发送到开发者或任何第三方。

### 4. 数据存储与保留

VolumeLock 不保存用户个人数据，因此不存在个人数据保留期限。

应用退出后，音量锁定会自动解除。应用不维护云端账户、云端配置或远程数据副本。

### 5. 第三方服务

VolumeLock 不集成第三方广告、分析、追踪或数据共享 SDK。

如果用户通过 Microsoft Store 安装或更新本应用，Microsoft 可能会按照 Microsoft 自身的隐私声明处理安装、更新、购买或诊断相关信息。此类信息由 Microsoft 控制，VolumeLock 不会接收或访问其中的个人数据。

### 6. 儿童隐私

VolumeLock 不收集任何人的个人信息，包括儿童的个人信息。

### 7. 安全

由于本应用不收集、不存储、不传输个人信息，也不运营用于存储用户数据的服务器，因此不存在由本应用产生的个人数据传输或存储风险。

### 8. 政策变更

如果本应用的隐私实践发生变化，我们会更新本文件并修改“最后更新”日期。更新后的政策将在发布后生效。

## English

### 1. Information We Collect

VolumeLock does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal information.

Specifically, the App:

- does not require an account or sign-in;
- does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, addresses, or other personally identifiable information;
- does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, telemetry, or behavioral profiling;
- does not collect crash logs or diagnostic data;
- does not read, record, save, or upload audio content;
- does not send data to any server.

### 2. Audio Device Access

To provide speaker and microphone volume control and locking, VolumeLock enumerates local Windows audio endpoints and reads or sets the system volume of the selected device.

The App may declare microphone device capability in order to identify and adjust microphone endpoint volume. However, the App does not capture, record, store, or transmit any audio content from the microphone.

### 3. Local Processing

All functionality is performed locally on the user's device. Volume settings, lock state, and tray interactions do not leave the device and are not sent to the developer or any third party.

### 4. Data Storage and Retention

VolumeLock does not store personal data, so there is no personal data retention period.

Volume locks are released when the App exits. The App does not maintain cloud accounts, cloud configuration, or remote copies of user data.

### 5. Third-Party Services

VolumeLock does not integrate third-party advertising, analytics, tracking, or data-sharing SDKs.

If the App is installed or updated through the Microsoft Store, Microsoft may process installation, update, purchase, or diagnostic information under Microsoft's own privacy statement. Such information is controlled by Microsoft, and VolumeLock does not receive or access personal data from it.

### 6. Children's Privacy

VolumeLock does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

### 7. Security

Because the App does not collect, store, or transmit personal information and does not operate a server for storing user data, there is no personal data transmission or storage created by the App.

### 8. Changes to This Policy

If the App's privacy practices change, we will update this document and revise the "Last updated" date. The updated policy will take effect when published.
